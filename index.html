Marks file

Grade a course in three steps

  1. Upload the marks file exported from your course records, or try the sample data.
  2. Set the grade cutoffs. Type a value or drag the markers on the distribution chart. Students close to a cutoff are listed so you can review them.
  3. Review and download the final grades as CSV or Excel.
